FAQ

What is the minimum purchase for a tax refund in Korea?

₩15,000 per receipt (one payment at one store) at shops in the tax refund scheme. Some stores set their own higher minimum, so check at the counter.

How much tax do tourists get back in Korea?

Prices include 10% VAT, which is ₩1 in every ₩11 you pay (9.09%). After the refund company's fee you usually get about 6–8% of the price back — for example around ₩6,000–8,000 on a ₩100,000 purchase.

What is an immediate (instant) tax refund?

At participating stores the VAT is taken off at the till when you show your passport. It works for receipts under ₩1,000,000, up to ₩5,000,000 in total per trip. Bigger purchases are refunded at the airport or a refund kiosk.

Do I need to show my purchases at the airport?

For airport refunds, keep the goods unused with the receipts and get them checked at the customs or kiosk before check-in if asked. Goods must leave Korea within 3 months of purchase.
