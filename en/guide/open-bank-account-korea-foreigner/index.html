How to Open a Bank Account in Korea as a Foreigner (2026): Documents, Limited Accounts and Tips

Korea Calculators · Updated 2026-11-24

At a glance
  • Bring your alien registration card (ARC), passport, a Korean phone number in your own name and proof of why you need the account (employment certificate or school enrollment).
  • New accounts usually start as limited accounts (한도제한계좌) with a low daily cap on transfers and ATM withdrawals — this is an anti-fraud rule, not a problem with you.
  • Show proof of employment or salary deposits later to lift the limit.
  • Get your account before your first payday: Korean employers pay salary by bank transfer.

What to bring

DocumentWhy
Alien registration card (ARC)Most banks require it for a regular account. You can apply for it within 90 days of arrival.
PassportIdentity check; your name must match the ARC exactly.
Korean mobile number in your nameNeeded for text verification, mobile banking and card payments online.
Proof of purposeEmployment certificate (재직증명서), employment contract or school enrollment certificate. It decides how high your limit can be.

Some banks open a passport-only account for new arrivals without an ARC, but it is usually more restricted. Visit a branch near your workplace or school — branches in areas with many foreign residents often have English or other language support.

Limited accounts and how to lift the limit

  • To stop voice-phishing and "money mule" fraud, Korean banks open most new accounts as limited accounts when the purpose cannot be fully proven.
  • The daily cap on online transfers and ATM withdrawals is typically a few hundred thousand won up to about ₩1 million, and higher at the counter. The exact amount depends on the bank — ask when you open the account.
  • To lift it, bring documents that match the account's purpose, such as an employment certificate, a payslip, or records of salary deposits. Many banks lift it once your salary has arrived a few times.

Branch banks vs internet banks

  • Branch banks (KB Kookmin, Shinhan, Woori, Hana, IBK, NH): you open the account in person. Many have foreigner-focused branches and apps in English.
  • Internet banks (Toss Bank, KakaoBank, K Bank): fully online and convenient, but you usually need an ARC and a Korean phone number in your name. Check each app's current rules for foreign residents.
  • For sending money home, banks and licensed remittance apps both work; compare the exchange rate and fee, and register your overseas transfer purpose if asked.
Never lend or sell your bank account or card to anyone. Doing so is a crime in Korea, can lead to fines or deportation, and is the main reason accounts get frozen.

FAQ

Can a foreigner open a bank account in Korea without an ARC?

Some banks open a limited account with a passport only, but most require an alien registration card for a regular account. If you just arrived, ask your bank which option it offers.

Why is my new Korean bank account limited?

New accounts are often opened as limited accounts (한도제한계좌) to prevent fraud. The daily transfer and ATM caps are lifted when you show proof of purpose, such as an employment certificate or salary deposits.

Can I use Toss or KakaoBank as a foreigner?

Resident foreigners can usually open internet bank accounts online if they have an alien registration card and a Korean phone number in their own name. Rules change, so check the app's current requirements.

Do I need a Korean bank account to get paid?

In practice yes. Korean employers pay wages by bank transfer, and you need a Korean account for most bills, phone plans and online payments.
