Korea Public Holidays 2027: Full List, Substitute Holidays and Long Weekends

Korea Calculators · Updated 2026-11-18

At a glance
  • Korea has 15 weekday public holidays in 2027, including 7 substitute holidays for holidays that fall on a weekend.
  • Seollal (Lunar New Year) is Feb 6–9 and Chuseok is Sep 14–16.
  • Labor Day (May 1) and Constitution Day (July 17) became official public holidays in 2026, so 2027 is the first year both get a substitute day off.
  • With 2–4 days of annual leave you can get 9–10 day breaks at Seollal, Chuseok and in early October.

A substitute holiday (대체공휴일) is the next working day off when a holiday falls on a weekend or overlaps another holiday. It applies to Seollal, Chuseok, Children's Day, Buddha's Birthday, Christmas and the national days — but not to New Year's Day or Memorial Day. No national election is scheduled in 2027.

Flights and trains fill up fast around Seollal and Chuseok. Korail sells holiday train tickets a few weeks ahead, and many shops and restaurants close on the day itself.

Do workers get public holidays off?

  • Workplaces with 5 or more employees: public holidays (including substitute days) are paid days off by law, for Korean and foreign workers alike.
  • If you work on a public holiday at such a workplace, you get 1.5x pay for up to 8 hours and 2x beyond that — use the overtime pay calculator.
  • Workplaces with fewer than 5 employees are not required to give public holidays off; it depends on your contract.
  • Public holidays are separate from your annual leave, so they are not deducted from it.
Labor Day (May 1) used to be a day off only for private-sector employees. Since the 2026 law change it is a public holiday for everyone, including public servants and teachers.

FAQ

When is Seollal (Lunar New Year) in 2027?

Seollal is Sunday, February 7, 2027. The holiday runs Feb 6–8, and because it overlaps a Sunday, Tuesday Feb 9 is a substitute holiday — four days off in a row.

When is Chuseok in 2027?

Chuseok is Wednesday, September 15, 2027. The holiday is Sep 14–16 (Tue–Thu). Taking leave on Monday Sep 13 and Friday Sep 17 gives you nine days off.

How many public holidays does Korea have in 2027?

There are 15 public holidays that fall on weekdays in 2027, 7 of them substitute holidays. For a Monday–Friday worker that means 119 days off in total including weekends.

Is Labor Day a public holiday in Korea?

Yes. Workers' Day was renamed Labor Day (노동절) and made an official public holiday in 2026. In 2027 it falls on a Saturday, so Monday May 3 is a substitute holiday.
