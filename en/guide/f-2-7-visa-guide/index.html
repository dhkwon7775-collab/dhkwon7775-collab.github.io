F-2-7 Visa Guide 2026: Who Can Apply, the 80-Point Rule, Documents and Path to F-5

Korea Calculators · Updated 2026-11-24

At a glance
  • F-2-7 is a residence visa for skilled foreigners who score at least 80 points.
  • Points come from age (up to 25), education (up to 25), Korean language (up to 20) and annual income (up to 60), plus bonuses.
  • It lets you change jobs more freely than an E-7 work visa, and family members can usually join you.
  • After years of stable stay on F-2-7 you may qualify for permanent residence (F-5).

Who can apply

  • Usually foreigners who have lived in Korea lawfully for at least 1 year on a professional status such as E-1 to E-7 (professors, teachers, researchers, specially designated activities).
  • Graduates of Korean universities and some other groups (for example workers in promising industries) can also qualify under separate rules.
  • You must meet the basic conditions too: no serious criminal record, taxes paid, and lawful stay.

How the points work

CategoryMaxEasiest way to add points
Age25Fixed — 25–29 scores highest
Education25STEM degrees score more; a Korean degree adds bonus points
Korean language (TOPIK / KIIP)20Each level adds 2–5 points; level 5 gives the full 20
Annual taxable income60Income from ₩30 million earns 30 points, ₩50 million 45, ₩100 million 60
Bonus—KIIP stage 5 (+10), ministry recommendation (+20), volunteering, top universities

Example: a 31-year-old with a bachelor's degree, TOPIK 4 and ₩45 million income scores 23 + 15 + 15 + 40 = 93 points. Use the F-2-7 points calculator for your own score.

Documents and where to apply

  • Typical documents: application form, passport, ARC, photo, income certificate (소득금액증명) and tax payment certificate, degree certificate, Korean language certificate, and proof of residence. Foreign documents usually need an apostille and translation.
  • Book an appointment on HiKorea and apply at your local immigration office. A change-of-status fee applies.
  • Prepare the income certificate early — immigration counts last year's taxable income, not your contract salary.
The points table and eligibility rules are set by the Ministry of Justice and change from time to time. Check HiKorea or call 1345 (immigration contact center, multilingual) before applying.

FAQ

What is the F-2-7 visa?

It is Korea's points-based residence visa. Skilled foreigners who score at least 80 points on age, education, Korean language and income (plus bonuses) can switch to it and stay with more freedom to change jobs.

How many years do I need in Korea for F-2-7?

Usually at least 1 year of lawful stay on an eligible professional status such as E-1 to E-7, though some routes have different conditions. Confirm your route with immigration.

Is TOPIK required for F-2-7?

Not strictly — you can reach 80 points without Korean. But Korean language is worth up to 20 points, and many applicants need it to pass.

Can F-2-7 lead to permanent residence?

Yes. After a period of stable stay on F-2-7 with sufficient income and other conditions, you may apply for F-5 permanent residence.
