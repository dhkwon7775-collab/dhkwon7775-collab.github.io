Korean Payslip Explained: 4 Major Insurances and Taxes on Your Salary (2026)

Korea Calculators · Updated 2026-10-25

In short
  • Four social insurances and two taxes come out of every Korean paycheck.
  • In 2026 employees pay about 9.7% of salary in social insurance, plus income tax.
  • Meal allowances up to ₩200,000 a month are tax-free and insurance-free.
  • Many foreign employees can get their National Pension back as a lump sum when they leave Korea.

The lines on your payslip

A Korean payslip (급여명세서) has two halves: what you earned (지급 내역) and what was taken out (공제 내역). Here is what the common Korean labels mean.

National Pension is charged on income between ₩410,000 and ₩6,590,000 a month (July 2026 – June 2027), so above that cap the pension deduction stops growing. The pension rate is rising every year until it reaches 13% (6.5% for you) in 2033.

Notes for foreign employees

Getting your National Pension back

When you leave Korea for good you may be able to claim a lump-sum refund (반환일시금) of your pension contributions plus interest. In general it is available if:

  • you worked on an E-8, E-9 or H-2 visa (regardless of nationality), or
  • your country has a social security agreement with Korea that allows it, or
  • your country gives Koreans the same right (reciprocity).

Some nationalities are instead exempt from paying in the first place. Ask the National Pension Service (1355, foreign-language support available) before you leave — there is also a counter at Incheon Airport.

Health insurance

Employees are covered through work. Foreign residents who are not covered at work generally have to join as local subscribers after living in Korea for six months.

Employment insurance

Whether it is mandatory depends on your visa. For example, residents with F-2, F-5 or F-6 status are generally covered automatically, while many work visas such as E-9 can join optionally. Being insured is what lets you claim unemployment benefits later, so it is worth checking with your employer.

A flat income-tax option

Foreign employees may be able to choose a flat 19% income tax rate on total pay instead of the normal progressive rates. It usually only helps high earners — compare both at year-end tax settlement.

Year-end tax settlement

The tax taken each month is an estimate. Every January–February your employer recalculates the year's tax using deductions such as card spending, insurance, medical bills and rent, and you either get money back or pay a little more in your February or March pay.

FAQ

How much of my salary goes to insurance in Korea?

In 2026 about 9.7% for employees: 4.75% pension, 3.595% health, about 0.47% long-term care and 0.9% employment insurance. Income tax is extra.

Why does my net pay change in April?

Health insurance is recalculated every April using last year's actual pay. If your pay went up, you may see an extra deduction that month.

Is the meal allowance taxed?

Up to ₩200,000 a month of meal allowance is tax-free and not charged social insurance, which slightly raises your take-home pay.
