Exchanging a Foreign Driver's License in Korea (2026): Documents, Fees and Steps

Korea Calculators · Updated 2026-11-24

At a glance
  • If your license comes from a country Korea recognises, you can exchange it at a driver's license test center without a road test.
  • Bring your original license, an embassy certificate or apostille for it, your passport, ARC and three photos.
  • Fees are small: a simple physical (eye) check of about ₩6,000 and a license card of ₩10,000 (₩15,000 for a mobile IC card).
  • Until then, you can drive on an international driving permit for up to a year from entry.

Step by step

  1. Check your country on the Korea Road Traffic Authority (KoROAD) list at safedriving.or.kr. Licenses from recognised countries can be exchanged; others must take Korean license tests.
  2. Get your license confirmed — either a certificate from your country's embassy in Korea or an apostille from home. Some embassies no longer issue these, so check early; apostilles must be arranged in your home country.
  3. Visit a driver's license test center (운전면허시험장) with your documents. You take a simple eye/physical check on site.
  4. Receive your Korean license, usually the same day. Your foreign license is usually kept and returned when you hand the Korean one back.

Good to know

  • An international driving permit (IDP) is valid in Korea for up to one year from your entry date. After you register as a resident, plan to exchange your license.
  • The license class you receive depends on your original license — most car licenses become a Class 2 ordinary license.
  • Requirements change by country and embassy. Call the test center or 1577-1120 (KoROAD) before you go.

FAQ

Can I exchange my foreign driver's license in Korea?

Yes, if your country is on Korea's recognised list. You exchange it at a driver's license test center with the original license, an embassy certificate or apostille, your passport, ARC and photos — no road test is needed.

How much does it cost to exchange a driver's license in Korea?

Roughly ₩16,000–₩21,000 in total: about ₩6,000 for the physical check and ₩10,000 (standard) or ₩15,000 (mobile IC) for the license card, plus the cost of photos and documents.

Can I drive in Korea with an international driving permit?

Yes, for up to one year from your date of entry, together with your original license. For longer stays, exchange your license for a Korean one.

What if my country is not on the list?

You need to get a Korean license the normal way: a safety course, written test, skills test and road test at a driving school or test center.
