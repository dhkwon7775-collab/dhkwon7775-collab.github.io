E-9 Worker Salary in Korea 2026: Monthly Pay, Overtime, Deductions and EPS Insurance

Korea Calculators · Updated 2026-11-12

At a glance
  • E-9 workers get the same minimum wage as Korean workers: ₩10,320 an hour and ₩2,156,880 a month (209 hours) in 2026, rising to ₩10,700 in 2027.
  • Overtime, night work and holiday work are paid at 1.5x (or more) at workplaces with 5+ employees — this is often a big part of E-9 pay.
  • Typical deductions are national pension, health insurance and income tax — around 10–11% of gross pay.
  • Your employer must also join you to the EPS insurances, including departure guarantee insurance, which is paid to you when you leave Korea.

What is deducted from E-9 pay

  • National pension (4.75%): depends on your home country's agreement with Korea. If it applies, you can usually claim a lump-sum refund when you leave.
  • Health insurance (3.595%) and long-term care: required for employees.
  • Employment insurance: for E-9, the unemployment benefit part is generally optional (only if you apply), so 0.9% is often not deducted. See unemployment benefits for foreigners.
  • Income tax: withheld monthly and settled each year in the year-end tax settlement.
  • Dormitory and meals: an employer can deduct these only if you agreed in writing, within limits set by Ministry of Employment and Labor guidelines (the guidelines were revised in 2025). Check your contract, and call 1350 if the amount looks too high.

If your pay is late or overtime is missing, keep your payslips and a record of your hours, then call the Employment and Labor helpline 1350 (interpretation available) or visit a Foreign Workers Support Center.

FAQ

Is there a different minimum wage for E-9 workers?

No. The Minimum Wage Act applies to all workers in Korea regardless of nationality or visa, so E-9 workers receive at least ₩10,320 an hour in 2026.

Can my employer pay me less because I live in the company dormitory?

No. Housing and meals are not part of the minimum wage. An employer may deduct dormitory or meal costs only with your written agreement and within the Ministry's guideline limits.

Do I get severance pay as an E-9 worker?

Yes, if you work for one year or more. For E-9 workers it is mostly paid through departure guarantee insurance when you leave Korea; if your severance under the law is higher, the employer must pay the difference.

How much do E-9 workers earn in a month?

With a 40-hour week only, about ₩2.16 million before deductions in 2026. With regular overtime, many earn ₩2.5–3 million or more before deductions.
