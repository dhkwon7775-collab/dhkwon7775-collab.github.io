Korea Income Tax Rates 2026: Tax Brackets 6–45%, How Salary Tax Is Calculated and Examples

Korea Calculators · Updated 2026-11-19

At a glance
  • Korea taxes income at 6% to 45% in eight brackets, plus local income tax of 10% of the income tax (so the top combined rate is 49.5%).
  • The brackets apply to your tax base, not your salary — big deductions and credits come first, so the real (effective) rate is much lower.
  • A single worker on ₩50 million a year pays about ₩2.59 million in income + local tax: an effective rate of about 5.2%.
  • Foreign workers can choose a flat 19% (20.9% with local tax) instead, which only helps at high salaries.

How tax on a salary is calculated

  1. Gross pay — your salary minus tax-free items such as a meal allowance of up to ₩200,000 a month.
  2. Earned income deduction — a sliding deduction for all employees (70% of the first ₩5 million, then smaller shares, capped at ₩20 million).
  3. Personal deductions — ₩1.5 million for you and each dependant, plus your National Pension and health/employment insurance contributions.
  4. Apply the brackets to what is left (the tax base).
  5. Subtract tax credits — the earned income tax credit, child credits, and credits for things like pension savings, insurance, medical and education costs in the year-end settlement.

Your employer withholds an estimate every month and settles the exact amount in the year-end tax settlement the following February.

The 19% flat rate for foreign workers

Foreign employees can choose to pay a flat 19% of gross pay (20.9% with local tax) with no deductions, for up to 20 years from their first day of work in Korea (if they started by December 31, 2026). Because the normal effective rate is lower for most salaries, the flat rate usually only saves money from roughly ₩165 million a year for a single person. FAQ

What is the income tax rate in Korea?

Korea's income tax rates run from 6% to 45% in eight brackets, plus a local income tax of 10% of the income tax. Because of deductions and credits, most salaried workers pay an effective rate well under 10%.

Is income tax in Korea calculated on my whole salary?

No. The brackets apply to the tax base after the earned income deduction, personal deductions and social insurance contributions, and tax credits are then subtracted from the result.

Do foreigners pay the same income tax as Koreans?

Yes, resident foreigners use the same brackets and deductions. They can also choose a flat 19% rate instead, which mostly benefits high earners.

When is income tax paid in Korea?

Employers withhold it from each month's pay using the simplified tax table and settle the exact amount in the year-end tax settlement the following February.
