Annual Leave in Korea: How Many Days You Get, Unused Leave Pay and Part-time Rules

Korea Calculators · Updated 2026-11-19

At a glance
  • First year: 1 paid day for each full month worked (up to 11 days).
  • After one year: 15 days, then 1 more day every 2 years, up to 25 days.
  • Unused leave usually becomes unused leave pay, and leave left when you quit must be paid out.
  • These rules apply at workplaces with 5 or more employees, to Korean and foreign workers alike.

How many leave days you get

Years workedAnnual leave
Under 1 year1 day per full month (max 11)
1–2 years15 days
3–4 years16 days
5–6 years17 days
11–12 years20 days
21 years or more25 days (maximum)

Leave is counted from your own start date. Many companies use a calendar-year system instead for convenience, but they may not give you fewer days than the start-date method would.

The 80% attendance rule

From year two, the 15+ days require that you attended at least 80% of working days in the previous year. Days off for work injuries, maternity leave, parental leave and annual leave itself count as attended. If you fall below 80%, you still get 1 day for each month with full attendance.

Unused leave and leaving your job

  • Leave must normally be used within one year. Days left over turn into unused leave pay: daily ordinary wage × unused days. Daily ordinary wage = monthly ordinary wage ÷ 209 × 8.
  • If your employer formally pushed you to use your leave (written notice under the leave-use promotion system) and you still did not, the pay may not be owed.
  • When you quit, all leave you have earned but not used must be paid out with your final pay, within 14 days.
  • Example: monthly ordinary wage ₩3,000,000 → daily ₩114,833; 5 unused days = about ₩574,000 before tax.

Part-time workers

Part-timers who work 15 hours or more a week earn leave in proportion to their hours: 15 days × (weekly hours ÷ 40) × 8 hours. Someone working 20 hours a week gets 15 × 20/40 × 8 = 60 hours of paid leave a year. Under 15 hours a week there is no annual leave.

Public holidays are separate

Public holidays and substitute holidays are paid days off on top of annual leave at workplaces with 5+ employees. See Korea public holidays 2027 to plan long breaks.

FAQ

How many vacation days do you get in Korea?

In the first year you get 1 paid day per full month worked (up to 11). From one year of service you get 15 days, rising by 1 day every two years to a maximum of 25 days.

Is annual leave paid in Korea?

Yes. Annual leave is paid at your ordinary wage. If you do not use it within the year, you are usually paid for the unused days.

Do I get my unused leave paid when I quit?

Yes. Leave you earned but did not use must be paid with your final wages, normally within 14 days of leaving.

Does annual leave apply to small companies?

The statutory annual leave rules apply only to workplaces with 5 or more regular employees. Smaller workplaces may offer leave by contract.
