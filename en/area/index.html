FAQ

How many m² is 1 pyeong?

1 pyeong is 400/121 m², about 3.3058 m². To convert m² to pyeong, divide by 3.3058 (or multiply by 0.3025).

Why do listings show both?

Since 2007 official documents must use m², but people and many listings still talk in pyeong, so you will often see both.
