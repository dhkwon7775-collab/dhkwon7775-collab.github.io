FAQ

When does night pay start in Korea?

Night work is any work between 10 pm and 6 am. At workplaces with 5 or more employees you get an extra 50% of your hourly wage for those hours.

What if overtime and night work overlap?

Both premiums apply. An hour of overtime at 11 pm is paid at 1 + 0.5 (overtime) + 0.5 (night) = 2 times your hourly wage.

Do E-9 and other foreign workers get overtime pay?

Yes. The Labor Standards Act applies to all workers in Korea regardless of visa, including E-9, H-2 and part-time students. Check that overtime and night pay appear as separate lines on your payslip.

What about workplaces with fewer than 5 employees?

The 50% premiums do not apply, but you must still be paid your hourly wage for every hour worked, and weekly holiday pay if you work 15+ hours a week.

Where can I get help if overtime is unpaid?

Call the Ministry of Employment and Labor at 1350 (interpretation available) or file a complaint online. Foreign workers can also contact a Foreign Workers Support Center.
