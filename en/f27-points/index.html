FAQ

How many points do I need for the F-2-7 visa?

You need at least 80 points. The main categories are age (up to 25), education (up to 25), Korean language (up to 20) and annual income (up to 60), plus bonus points and deductions.

Which income counts for F-2-7 points?

Your taxable income for the previous year as shown on your income certificate (소득금액증명) from the National Tax Service. Tax-free allowances are not included.

Is TOPIK or KIIP better for F-2-7?

Both give the same points per level (3, 5, 10, 15 and 20 points for levels 1–5). TOPIK results are valid for 2 years; completing KIIP stage 5 also adds 10 bonus points.

Is this calculator official?

No. It follows the published points table as of November 2026, but immigration updates the rules. Confirm your score and eligibility with HiKorea or the 1345 immigration call center.
